## Styling and Care Tips

Maintaining the pristine condition of your winter garments ensures they will remain staples in your wardrobe for years to come. High-end materials like wool, cashmere, and specialized technical fabrics require targeted care routines.

* **Proper Storage:** Store heavy knits folded rather than on hangers to prevent stretching, and keep shearling items in breathable garment bags away from direct heat sources.
* **Post-Snow Care:** After a day in the snow, always let wet outerwear dry naturally in a well-ventilated room. Avoid using direct heaters or hair dryers, which can warp specialized fabrics and ruin natural furs or shearling.
* **Layering Balance:** If you are wearing a voluminous piece like an oversized puffer or a statement jacket with toscana tuxedo details, balance the silhouette with slimmer bottoms, such as thermal leggings or tailored ski trousers.
* **Color Coordination:** Anchor bright, neon statement pieces with neutral base layers in cream, black, or navy to keep the look grounded and sophisticated.
